OpenDP's make_bounded_sum accepts vectors of any length with i32 values in [0, 2^30]. The output is their signed i32 sum, and its stability claim for one inserted row element is 2^30. Check neighboring datasets only: insertion distance zero or one.

Use these four public vectors: [2^30 - 1], [2^30, 2^30 - 1], [2^30, 2^30 - 1, 1], and [2^30, 2^30 - 1, 0]. Compare their compiled sums as mathematical signed integers. The required contract covers every pair of these vectors and insertion distance zero or one. The model proof should state whether the compiled sum satisfies that bound.
